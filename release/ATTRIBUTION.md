# Attribution

The models in this release are built from open data:

- **Map data** © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence
  (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). The city model is a derived work.
- **Street levels**: Ordnance Survey of Jerusalem, 1864-65, by Capt. C. W. Wilson R.E. (public
  domain), from the Princeton University Library scan.
- **Elevation**: AWS Terrain Tiles (https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), SRTM-derived.
- **Textures**: Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0.
- **Photographs** shown with **P** are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Each is shown with its
  author and licence, and links to its Commons page.
